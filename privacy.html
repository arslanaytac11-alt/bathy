Privacy Policy — BATHY

Last updated: October 10, 2026

BATHY has no accounts and no servers. Your progress is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Ads

BATHY shows ads through Google AdMob (optional rewarded videos and ads between dives). Google may collect approximate location derived from your IP address, device identifiers (the advertising identifier / IDFA only if you allow tracking), ad interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can change your tracking choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland your ad choices are requested through Google's consent form and can be changed from the game's Settings screen. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Photos

Photos you take in photo mode are created on your device and are only shared if you choose to share or save them.

Children

BATHY is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
